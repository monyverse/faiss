# Project Baobab — Confidential Information Memorandum

## The opportunity
The shareholders of Savanna Commercial Bank PLC ("Savanna") are exploring the sale of 100% of the bank. Savanna is a mid-sized commercial bank in Kenya with 62 branches and around 1.4 million customers, focused on SME and retail lending.

## Licence and regulatory standing
Savanna holds a banking licence issued by the Central Bank of Kenya and is subject to its prudential supervision. The most recent supervisory inspection was completed in 2025; management reports that remediation of two findings on credit classification is in progress.

## Technology
Savanna runs its core banking platform on a vendor system implemented in 2017, with a mobile banking application built in-house. A disaster recovery site is maintained in a second data centre and a business continuity test was run in 2025. Card processing and the ATM switch are outsourced to third party service providers under contracts renewing in 2027.

## People
The bank employs approximately 2,100 employees. Around 40% are members of a banking union and are covered by a collective agreement.
